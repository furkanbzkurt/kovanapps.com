🗺 Crime Map: Local Safety Alerts

Data Sources

Every source of the information shown in Crime Map, with a link to the original publisher. Each incident in the app also links to the page it came from.

Crime Map is an independent app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by any law-enforcement agency, 911 center or government body. Data is shown for information only, may be incomplete or delayed, and does not replace emergency services. In an emergency, call 911.

National

  • Crime statistics — FBI Crime Data Explorer: cde.ucr.cjis.gov
  • Severe-weather alerts — U.S. National Weather Service (NOAA): weather.gov/alerts
