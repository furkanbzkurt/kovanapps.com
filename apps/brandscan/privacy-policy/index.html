This page informs visitors regarding our policies with the collection, use, and disclosure of Personal Information if anyone decides to use our Service. By using our Service, you agree to the collection and use of information in relation to this policy.

1. Information Collection and Use

For a better experience while using our Service, we may require you to provide certain information. The information we request will be retained on your device or processed temporarily on our servers and is not stored permanently.

1.1 Camera Data

BrandScan uses your device camera to capture images of products and brand logos for identification. These images are:

  • Processed on-device for preprocessing (compression, resizing)
  • Sent to our secure servers for AI-powered brand analysis
  • Not stored permanently — images are deleted immediately after analysis is complete
  • Never shared with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes

1.2 AI-Powered Analysis

BrandScan uses artificial intelligence (Google Gemini) to analyze product images and identify brands. The AI processes your images solely for the purpose of returning brand information to you. No image data is retained after analysis.

BrandScan offers optional premium subscriptions. Payment processing is handled entirely by the platform stores (Google Play, Apple App Store). We do not collect or store your payment information (credit card numbers, billing address, etc.).
