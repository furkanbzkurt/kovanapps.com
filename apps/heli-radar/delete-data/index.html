Delete Your Data

🗓 Effective: October 2026 🔄 Last Updated: October 2026

Delete in the App (instant)

Open Heli Radar → Settings → Data & privacy → Delete my data. This immediately deletes your saved places, alert preferences and push token from our server and clears them from your device.

Delete without the App

Uninstalling removes everything stored on the device. Server-side data tied to an uninstalled app is deleted automatically 30 days after it stops receiving notifications. To have it deleted sooner, email info@kovanapps.com with the subject “Heli Radar data deletion”. We complete requests within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Places (coordinates, radius, label) and alert preferences
  • Push token, platform and time zone
  • Our cached copy of your subscription status

What is kept

  • Purchase records held by Apple, Google and RevenueCat, as required to honour and restore purchases
  • Aggregate, anonymous crash and analytics data (up to 90 days / 14 months in Firebase)
