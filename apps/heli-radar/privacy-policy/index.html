Privacy Policy

🗓 Effective: October 2026 🔄 Last Updated: October 2026

Kovan Apps Yazılım Ltd. Şti. (“Company”, “we”, “us”) built the Heli Radar app (the “App”) for Google Play (Android) and the Apple App Store (iOS). The App shows ADS-B equipped helicopters near places you choose, tells you who operates them and whether they are circling, and can alert you. It is free to use; extra features are unlocked with an optional subscription or one-time purchase. There are no ads.

🏠 Your address stays on your phone. The App has no account and never asks for your name, email or phone number. The places you save (for example “Home”) are stored encrypted on your device. To send you alerts, our server keeps the place’s coordinates and radius — never a street address or a name you typed — tied only to an anonymous device ID.

1. No Account, No Sign-Up

Your device is identified only by an anonymous Firebase Authentication ID created on first launch. It identifies an app installation, not a person.

4. Aircraft Privacy

Aircraft enrolled in the FAA’s privacy programs (LADD, PIA) are never shown. For privately owned helicopters we never show an owner’s name.

5. Notifications

If you allow notifications, our server sends alerts about helicopters circling near your places, major incidents (Pro) and a weekly summary. You choose which classes and quiet hours apply in the App.

7. Your Rights and Deleting Your Data

  • In the App: Settings → Data & privacy → Delete my data deletes your places, alert preferences and push token from our server immediately and clears them from the device.
  • Without the App: see Delete Your Data.
  • You may request access to the data tied to your anonymous ID at info@kovanapps.com. We respond within 30 days.

8. Security

All traffic uses HTTPS/TLS. Backend access is limited to authenticated Cloud Functions protected by Firebase App Check; database rules deny all direct access. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

9. Children

Heli Radar is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. Contact info@kovanapps.com and we will delete it.
