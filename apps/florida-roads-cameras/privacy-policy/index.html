2. Traffic and Incident Data

The App displays real-time traffic camera images and incident reports sourced from the Florida Department of Transportation (FDOT) through the publicly accessible FL511 system. Specifically:

  • Camera images are loaded directly from FDOT’s public CDN on demand; images are not stored on our servers.
  • Incident reports (accidents, lane closures, construction, road work) are fetched from FDOT’s public DIVAS feed and cached briefly on our backend for performance.
  • This data is public record and contains no personally identifiable information.
  • We are not affiliated with FDOT and do not claim ownership of any traffic data.

3. Weather and Hurricane Data

The App provides weather warnings and hurricane tracking from the National Weather Service (NWS) and the National Hurricane Center (NHC). Specifically:

  • Weather alerts are fetched from the NWS public API and cached briefly for performance.
  • Hurricane advisories and forecast tracks are fetched from the NHC public feeds.
  • None of this data contains personal information.
  • Weather information is for informational purposes only and should not be used as the sole basis for emergency preparedness decisions. Always consult official sources and local emergency management.

4. Subscription Data

The App offers optional premium (“Pro”) subscriptions managed through RevenueCat. When you subscribe:

  • Payment is processed entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Play Store). We do not collect or store your payment details (credit card numbers, billing address, etc.).
  • RevenueCat receives an anonymous subscriber ID and purchase receipt to validate your subscription status.
  • We store your subscription status locally on your device to unlock premium features.

5. Push Notifications

The App offers opt-in push notifications via Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) for:

  • Severe traffic incidents on major Florida highways (I-95, I-75, I-4, I-10, Turnpike, etc.)
  • Bridge closures on selected major bridges (Sunshine Skyway, Howard Frankland, etc.)
  • Hurricane watches, warnings, and advisories for Florida
  • Storm surge warnings

Push notifications are delivered via topic subscriptions only. We do not collect, store, or associate your device token with any personal identifier. You may disable notifications at any time through your device’s system settings or within the App.

6. Location Data

The App may request access to your device’s approximate or precise location to center the map on your current position and show nearby traffic cameras. Location data is:

  • Used only locally on your device to display nearby cameras and centering the map.
  • Never stored on our servers.
  • Never shared with third parties.
  • Entirely optional — the App works without location access; the map simply defaults to a statewide Florida view.

10. Advertising and Tracking

The App is ad-supported for free users. Ads are served by Google AdMob. AdMob may use your device’s advertising identifier and other non-personal data to serve relevant ads.

iOS (Apple)

On iOS, the App uses Apple’s App Tracking Transparency (ATT) framework. Before any tracking occurs, you will be asked for permission via a system dialog. If you choose “Ask App Not to Track,” no tracking data will be collected for advertising purposes. You can change this at any time in:

Android

Pro subscribers do not see ads. Subscribing removes all advertising from the App.

12. Data Retention

We retain personal data only as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy:

  • Crash logs and analytics: Retained by Firebase for up to 90 days.
  • Traffic and weather cache: Automatically invalidated within minutes on our servers; no long-term storage.

  • Subscription data: Retained by RevenueCat for the duration of your subscription and up to 30 days after cancellation.
  • Favorites, camera groups, and preferences: Stored locally on your device and deleted when you uninstall the App.
