The crime, incident, and weather information in the App is aggregated from publicly available third-party data sources. Because this data is public, there is no licensing restriction on its display; however, we do not own, control, or guarantee the accuracy, completeness, availability, or timeliness of any of it. Sources include:

  • City & county open-data portals — official police / incident datasets published by U.S. cities and counties (many powered by open-data platforms such as Socrata / Tyler Technologies and ArcGIS).
  • Public safety / 911 dispatch feeds — publicly published computer-aided-dispatch (CAD) and active-call feeds where a jurisdiction makes them available.
  • U.S. National Weather Service (NOAA / NWS) — official severe-weather watches, warnings, and advisories.

Coverage, detail, and reporting speed vary by jurisdiction. Some areas publish in near real time, others on a daily schedule, and some publish no data at all. Sources may change, be delayed, or be discontinued at any time.
