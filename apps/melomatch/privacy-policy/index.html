MeloMatch

Privacy Policy

Effective: December 4, 2024 Last Updated: November 5, 2026

Kovan Apps Yazılım Ltd. Şti. ("Kovan Apps", "Company", "we", "us", or "our") operates the MeloMatch mobile application ("App", "Service"), available on Google Play (Android) and the Apple App Store (iOS). MeloMatch is a music-first social app: you build a profile from the artists and songs you love, discover people who share your taste, challenge them to music duels, and start conversations with a song. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your personal information when you use the App on either platform.

YOUR PRIVACY MATTERS: We are committed to handling your data transparently. This Privacy Policy is written to comply with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law (KVKK), the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), and other applicable data protection laws. The App contains no advertising, and we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

By using MeloMatch, you consent to the data practices described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with our policies and practices, please do not use the App.

1.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Location Data: With your permission we read your device's approximate (city-level) location while the App is in use (Android: approximate location only; iOS: "While Using the App" with reduced accuracy). You can also pick your city by name instead. We store these approximate coordinates and the derived city on our servers to show you people nearby and concerts in your area. Other users only see your city and an approximate distance, never your exact coordinates. Location is required for discovery; you can revoke it in device settings, in which case discovery will not work.
  • Device and App Information: Device model, operating system version, app version, language, time zone, a random app-instance identifier, and a push notification token (Firebase Cloud Messaging on Android, Apple Push Notification service on iOS).
  • Usage Data: Screens viewed, features used, connection requests sent and received, duels played, songs sent, subscription events. Collected through Google Analytics for Firebase.
  • Diagnostic Data: Crash reports and non-fatal errors (Firebase Crashlytics), including device state at the time of the error and your internal user ID so we can investigate reports you send us.
  • Security Signals: Device-attestation tokens (Firebase App Check, and Google reCAPTCHA Enterprise during phone verification on iOS) used only to block automated abuse. These do not identify you personally.

1.3 Information from Third Parties

  • Apple Music (iOS, optional): If you grant Media & Apple Music access, the App reads artists and songs from your library on your device to suggest favorites. Only the artists and songs you actually pick are sent to our servers, where they are resolved against the public music catalog. We never receive your Apple ID or your full listening history.
  • App Store and Google Play: Purchase receipts and subscription status, delivered to us through RevenueCat. We never see your payment card details.

1.4 Public Music and Event Data (Not About You)

To build your profile, duels and the concert feed, our servers query public catalogs: the Apple Music / iTunes catalog, the Spotify catalog, MusicBrainz (genres) and Ticketmaster (concerts). These queries are made from our servers, contain no personal identifiers, and are cached for all users. Song previews played in the App are 30-second clips served by Apple and remain the property of their rights holders.

2. How We Use Your Information

2.1 Core Service Functionality

We use your information to create and manage your account, verify your phone number, display your profile to other users, calculate music compatibility, rank who you see and who sees you, run duels, deliver songs and chat messages, show concerts near you, and process subscriptions.

2.2 Service Improvement and Analytics

We analyze aggregate usage to improve the App, develop new features, monitor performance, and find and fix technical problems.

2.3 Safety and Security

We screen every uploaded photo with the Google Cloud Vision API (SafeSearch) before it is shown, review reports filed by users, detect and prevent fraud and automated abuse, enforce our Terms, and comply with legal obligations.

2.4 Communication

We send push notifications about connection requests, duels, songs and messages you receive (you can turn these off in device settings), respond to support inquiries, and notify you of changes to our policies.

3. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR / KVKK)

  • Contractual Necessity: Processing needed to provide the App you signed up for (profile, discovery, duels, chat, subscriptions)
  • Consent: Location access, Apple Music library access, push notifications, and photo library access. You may withdraw consent at any time in device settings.
  • Legitimate Interests: Keeping the App safe, preventing fraud, improving the Service, and analyzing aggregate usage
  • Legal Obligations: Responding to lawful requests and reporting obligations

4. How We Share Your Information

WE DO NOT SELL YOUR PERSONAL DATA. We never sell, rent, or trade your personal information, and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers.

4.1 With Other Users

The following is visible to other MeloMatch users: your first name, age, gender, city and approximate distance, bio, photos, favorite artists and songs, genres, your compatibility score with them, duel results you played together, and songs you send. If you open someone's profile while it is featured in Spotlight, that person can see that you viewed it. Your phone number, exact location, date of birth and discovery preferences are never shown to other users.

4.3 Legal Requirements

We may disclose your information if required by law or valid legal process, to enforce our Terms, to protect the safety of users or the public, or to detect and prevent fraud. Reports of child sexual abuse material are forwarded to the appropriate authorities as described in our Terms.

4.4 Business Transfers

If Kovan Apps is involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred to the successor entity. We will notify you before your information becomes subject to a different privacy policy.

5. Data Security

All traffic between the App and our servers is encrypted with TLS. Our database is not directly accessible from the App: every read and write goes through server-side functions that verify your identity and validate the request, and backend access is protected by device attestation (Firebase App Check). Data is stored encrypted at rest on Google Cloud. Only a small number of Kovan Apps staff can access production data, and only for support, moderation and debugging.

Important Notice: No method of internet transmission or storage is 100% secure. Keep your phone and SIM secure, since your phone number is your sign-in credential.

6. Data Retention

  • Active accounts: Profile, music, connection and chat data are retained while your account is active.
  • Deleted accounts: Your profile is hidden immediately. Profile data, photos, connections and duels are deleted from our production systems within 30 days. Messages you already sent may remain in the other person's copy of the conversation. Limited records (for example moderation actions and the hashed identifier of a banned account) may be kept for up to 90 days, or longer where required by law, to prevent abuse and to handle legal claims.
  • Reports and moderation records: Retained for as long as needed to enforce our Terms and meet legal reporting obligations.
  • Crash logs: Retained by Firebase Crashlytics for up to 90 days.
  • Analytics events: Retained by Google Analytics for Firebase for up to 14 months.
  • Purchase records: Retained by RevenueCat, Apple and Google under their own policies to support purchase restoration and tax obligations.
  • Inactive accounts: We may delete accounts that have been inactive for more than 2 years after notifying you.

7.1 Rights Under GDPR and KVKK

You have the right to access, rectify and erase your personal data, to restrict or object to processing, to data portability, to withdraw consent at any time, and to lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority (in Turkey, the Personal Data Protection Authority, KVKK).

7.2 Rights Under CCPA (California Users)

You have the right to know what personal information we collect and how it is used, the right to delete it, the right to opt out of its sale or sharing (we do neither), and the right not to be discriminated against for exercising these rights.

7.3 How to Exercise Your Rights

ACCOUNT DELETION: You can delete your account at any time inside the App on both iOS and Android:
Profile tab → Settings → Account → Delete Account → Confirm
Or follow the steps on our Delete Account page.

To edit your profile, photos, music or discovery preferences, use the Profile tab. We respond within 30 days (GDPR/KVKK) or 45 days (CCPA).

We do not use advertising SDKs, and we do not share your data with advertising networks. On iOS, the App Store privacy "nutrition label" for MeloMatch reflects the practices in this Policy.
