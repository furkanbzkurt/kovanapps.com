Child Safety Standards

Last Updated: November 5, 2026. These standards apply to MeloMatch on Google Play and the App Store, published by Kovan Apps Yazılım Ltd. Şti.

1. Zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation

MeloMatch prohibits child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and child sexual abuse material (CSAM) of any kind. This includes any content that sexualizes minors, grooming, sextortion, trafficking, and any attempt to contact, solicit, or meet a minor. Violating accounts are permanently banned.

2. Adults only

MeloMatch is for people aged 18 and over. Date of birth is required at sign-up and the age limit is enforced on our servers. Accounts that appear to belong to a minor are removed. Profile photos must show the account holder and must not include children.

3. How we prevent and detect abuse

  • Every uploaded photo is screened automatically on our servers (Google Cloud Vision SafeSearch) before it can be added to a profile; rejected photos are deleted.
  • Profile text is checked on our servers before it is saved.
  • Every profile and conversation has in-app Report and Block.
  • Reports are reviewed by our team within 24 hours. Child safety reports are marked urgent and reviewed first.

4. How to report

In the app: open the profile or conversation, tap Report, and choose "Under 18 or child safety". Reports are confidential; the reported person is not told who reported them.

By email: write to info@kovanapps.com with the subject "Child safety". This is our designated child safety contact.

If a child is in immediate danger, contact your local police or emergency services first.

5. What we do with a report

We remove the content, permanently ban the account, preserve evidence as the law requires, and report apparent CSAM to the NCMEC CyberTipline (18 U.S.C. § 2258A) and to the competent authorities in the relevant country. We cooperate with law enforcement requests.

6. Compliance

We comply with applicable child safety laws in the countries where MeloMatch is available. See also our Terms of Service (section 3.1) and Privacy Policy.
