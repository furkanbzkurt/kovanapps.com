🗑️ Delete Your Data

Pennsylvania Roads & Cameras does not require an account to use. Most of your data never leaves your device. Here is how to delete any data that is stored locally or processed by our third-party services.

🔒 Good to know: We do not create user accounts, we do not collect your name, email, or phone number, and we do not store location data on our servers. Your camera favorites, groups, alert watches, and preferences are saved locally on your device only.

📱 Method 1 — Uninstall the App (Fastest)

All of your locally stored data is deleted automatically when you uninstall Pennsylvania Roads & Cameras from your device. This includes:

  • Camera favorites, custom groups, and alert watches
  • App preferences (theme, units, notification settings)
  • Locally cached camera images, incident data, and road condition data
  • Firebase anonymous authentication session

iOS: Long-press the app icon → Remove App → Delete App.
Android: Long-press the app icon → App info → Uninstall. Or go to Settings → Apps → Pennsylvania Roads & Cameras → Uninstall.

📧 Method 2 — Email a Data Deletion Request

For data that is held by our third-party service providers (crash reports, analytics, subscription records), email us and we will initiate deletion on your behalf.

  1. Send an email to info@kovanapps.com.
  2. Subject line: “Pennsylvania Roads & Cameras – Data Deletion Request”
  3. Include (optional) your Firebase Installation ID or RevenueCat App User ID if you know them. These are not required — we can process a deletion request without any identifier.
  4. If you are a subscriber, mention the store (Apple or Google) and the approximate subscription date.

We will respond within 30 days and confirm once deletion is complete.

📲 Method 3 — Reset Your Advertising ID

The App uses Google AdMob to display ads. AdMob uses your device's advertising identifier. You can reset or disable it at any time:

iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking → toggle off “Allow Apps to Request to Track”. You can also open the App, go to Settings, and respond “Ask App Not to Track” to the prompt.
Android: Settings → Google → Ads → “Delete advertising ID” or “Opt out of Ads Personalization”.

Pro subscribers do not see ads, so no advertising ID is used while subscribed.

🌍 Method 4 — Third-Party Data Deletion (Advanced)

You may also request deletion directly from each third-party service that the App uses:

  • Google (AdMob + Firebase): myaccount.google.com → Data & privacy → Delete a service or your account.
  • RevenueCat (subscription records): Email support@revenuecat.com with your subscription App User ID, or request we do it on your behalf via Method 2 above.
  • Apple (purchase records): These are held by Apple and can be requested via privacy.apple.com.

Note: we cannot delete data held by Apple or Google on our behalf.
