1. Description of Service

Pennsylvania Roads & Cameras is a free, ad-supported application that provides real-time access to publicly available traffic camera feeds, incident reports, road conditions, lane closures, active snow-plow locations, and RWIS road-surface sensor data, plus NWS weather warnings, for the state of Pennsylvania, USA. The App aggregates data from official public sources including the Pennsylvania Department of Transportation (PennDOT) through 511PA and the National Weather Service (NWS).
