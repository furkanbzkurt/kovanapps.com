1. Description of Service

Permit Hours is a supervised-driving log. It lets you record practice sessions — by timer or by entering them manually — split them into daytime and nighttime minutes, track progress toward your state’s stated requirements, share the log with another supervisor, and export a printable PDF report.

⚠️ Not an official DMV service: Permit Hours is an independent app and is NOT affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to any Department of Motor Vehicles, Bureau of Motor Vehicles, state licensing agency, insurer, or driving school. The hour requirements shown in the App are compiled from public sources for reference only and may be out of date or incomplete. Always verify current requirements with your own state’s licensing agency, and confirm with them whether a printed log from this App is acceptable before relying on it.
