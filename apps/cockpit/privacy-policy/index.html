Privacy Policy

🗓 Effective: October 2026 🔄 Last Updated: October 2026

Kovan Apps Yazılım Ltd. Şti. (“Company”, “we”, “us”) built the Cockpit: Live Flight Tracker app (the “App”) for Google Play (Android) and the Apple App Store (iOS). The App shows live aircraft on a map, airport weather and NOTAMs, aviation events such as emergency squawks, pilot (ACARS) messages, and public air traffic control audio. The map, aircraft details, search and airport cards are free; other features are unlocked with an optional subscription or one-time purchase. There are no ads.

✈️ No account, no ads, no tracking. The App never asks for your name, email or phone number. Your device is identified only by an anonymous ID. Your precise location is used on the device only, to centre the map; it is never stored on our server. We do not sell data, we do not use an advertising ID, and the App does not track you across other apps or websites.

1. No Account, No Sign-Up

Your device is identified only by an anonymous Firebase Authentication ID created on first launch. It identifies an app installation, not a person. The same anonymous ID is used by our backend, by RevenueCat for purchases and by Google Analytics for Firebase. You can see it in the App under Settings.

4. Aircraft and Crew Privacy

  • Aircraft enrolled in the FAA’s privacy programs (LADD, PIA) are never shown or stored.
  • For privately owned aircraft we never show an owner’s name, and we keep no registration history for private aircraft.
  • Crew names and personnel numbers are removed from pilot (ACARS) messages on our server before they are stored, displayed or sent to AI.

5. Notifications

If you allow notifications, our server sends alerts that match your rules (event types, region, airline, Home radius, favourite aircraft and airports) and an optional daily digest. You choose the level, types and quiet hours in the App.

7. Your Rights and Deleting Your Data

  • In the App: Settings → Data and privacy → Delete my data immediately removes your alert rules, followed flights, home region, Live Activities, feedback and devices from our servers and starts the App fresh with a new anonymous ID.
  • Without the App: see Delete Your Data.
  • You may request access to, or deletion of, the data tied to your anonymous ID at info@kovanapps.com. We respond within 30 days.
  • Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK or California), you may also have the right to object, restrict processing or complain to a supervisory authority.

8. Security

All traffic uses HTTPS/TLS. Backend access is limited to authenticated Cloud Functions protected by Firebase App Check, with rate limits and input validation; database rules deny all direct access. Pro status is verified on our server. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

9. Children

Cockpit is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. Contact info@kovanapps.com and we will delete it.
