1. Description of Service

Global Entry Slot Finder displays the soonest available Global Entry (Trusted Traveler Programs) interview appointments at US enrollment centers, using publicly available data from the official TTP scheduler. Browsing every center is free. An optional one-time Pro unlock adds appointment alerts: the App’s servers keep checking your chosen centers and send you a push notification when a matching appointment opens. Appointments are always booked by you, on the official ttp.cbp.dhs.gov website.

Appointment availability displayed in the App is sourced from the publicly accessible TTP scheduler and refreshed on a best-effort schedule (watched centers are typically checked about every 90 seconds; all other centers about every 20 minutes). By using the App, you acknowledge that:

  • Availability is not real-time — an appointment shown in the App may already be taken by the time you open the official website
  • Alert notifications may be delayed or occasionally not delivered due to network conditions, device power-saving modes, or push-infrastructure behavior outside our control
  • The public scheduler may change, throttle, or become unavailable without notice, which can degrade or interrupt the Service
  • “Last checked” timestamps in the App show exactly how fresh the displayed data is
