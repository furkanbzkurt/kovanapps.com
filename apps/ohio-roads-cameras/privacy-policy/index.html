2. Traffic, Incident, and Road Condition Data

The App displays real-time traffic camera images, incident reports, road conditions, construction notices, variable message signs, RWIS road-surface sensor readings, truck-parking availability, and travel-time information sourced from the Ohio Department of Transportation (ODOT) through the publicly accessible OHGO API. Specifically:

  • Camera images are loaded directly from ODOT’s public CDN on demand; images are not stored on our servers.
  • Incident reports, lane closures, road conditions, construction notices, RWIS sensor data, VMS messages, truck-parking availability, and travel times are fetched from ODOT’s public OHGO API and cached briefly on our backend for performance.
  • ODOT data is public record under the Ohio Public Records Act, Ohio Revised Code § 149.43.
  • None of this data contains personally identifiable information.
  • We are not affiliated with ODOT and do not claim ownership of any traffic data.

3. Weather Data

The App provides winter weather warnings and severe weather alerts sourced from the National Weather Service (NWS). Specifically:

  • Winter storm warnings, blizzard warnings, and weather alerts are fetched from the NWS public API.
  • None of this data contains personal information.
  • Weather and road condition information is for informational purposes only and should not be used as the sole basis for emergency preparedness decisions. Always consult official sources, local emergency management, and posted ODOT signage before traveling.

4. Subscription Data

The App offers optional premium (“Pro”) subscriptions managed through RevenueCat. When you subscribe:

  • Payment is processed entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Play Store). We do not collect or store your payment details (credit card numbers, billing address, etc.).
  • RevenueCat receives an anonymous subscriber ID and purchase receipt to validate your subscription status.
  • We store your subscription status locally on your device to unlock premium features.

5. Push Notifications

The App offers opt-in push notifications via Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) for:

  • Severe traffic incidents on major Ohio highways (I-70, I-71, I-75, I-77, I-80, I-90, I-270, I-275, I-475, I-480, I-670)
  • NWS winter storm warnings, blizzard warnings, and high-impact advisories for Ohio

Push notifications are delivered via topic subscriptions only. We do not collect, store, or associate your device token with any personal identifier. You may disable notifications at any time through your device’s system settings or within the App.

6. Location Data

The App may request access to your device’s approximate or precise location to center the map on your current position and show nearby traffic cameras. Location data is:

  • Used only locally on your device to display nearby cameras and incidents and to center the map.
  • Never stored on our servers.
  • Never shared with third parties.
  • Entirely optional — the App works without location access; the map simply defaults to a statewide Ohio view.

10. Advertising and Tracking

The App is ad-supported for free users. Ads are served by Google AdMob. AdMob may use your device’s advertising identifier and other non-personal data to serve relevant ads.

iOS (Apple)

On iOS, the App uses Apple’s App Tracking Transparency (ATT) framework. Before any tracking occurs, you will be asked for permission via a system dialog. If you choose “Ask App Not to Track,” no tracking data will be collected for advertising purposes. You can change this at any time in:

  • Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking

Android

On Android, you can opt out of personalized advertising through:

  • Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization

Pro subscribers do not see ads. Subscribing removes all advertising from the App.

11. Data Security

We value your trust in providing us your Personal Information and strive to use commercially acceptable means of protecting it. All network communication with our backend uses HTTPS/TLS encryption. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure and reliable, and we cannot guarantee its absolute security.

12. Data Retention

We retain personal data only as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy:

  • Crash logs and analytics: Retained by Firebase for up to 90 days.
  • Traffic, road condition, and weather cache: Automatically invalidated within minutes on our servers; no long-term storage.
  • Push notification history: We retain hashed incident identifiers for up to 48 hours to prevent duplicate notifications; these contain no personal data.
  • Subscription data: Retained by RevenueCat for the duration of your subscription and up to 30 days after cancellation.
  • Favorites, camera groups, and preferences: Stored locally on your device and deleted when you uninstall the App.
