2. Traffic Camera and Road Data

The App displays traffic camera snapshots, incident and closure reports, work zones and winter road conditions published by state departments of transportation (DOTs) through their public 511 systems. Specifically:

  • Camera images are loaded by your device directly from each DOT’s public servers on demand and refreshed about every 60 seconds; images are not stored on our servers.
  • Incident, closure, work-zone and road-condition data is fetched from public DOT feeds and cached briefly on our backend so that thousands of users do not hit the agencies’ servers at once.
  • Cameras that a DOT has marked offline are filtered out before they reach your device.
  • This data is public record and contains no personally identifiable information.
  • We are not affiliated with any DOT and do not claim ownership of any traffic data. Coverage is listed in the App under More → Data sources; states marked “coming soon” show national weather data only.

3. Weather and Hurricane Data

The App shows weather warnings from the National Weather Service (NWS), winter-storm severity from NOAA and hurricane tracking from the National Hurricane Center (NHC). Specifically:

  • Weather alerts, winter-storm outlooks and hurricane advisories are fetched from public NWS, NOAA and NHC feeds and cached briefly for performance.
  • When you open a camera, the App asks our backend for the warnings that apply to that camera’s location, never to your own position.
  • None of this data contains personal information.
  • Weather information is for informational purposes only and should not be used as the sole basis for travel or emergency decisions. Always consult official sources and local emergency management.

4. Subscription Data

The App offers optional premium (“Pro”) subscriptions managed through RevenueCat. When you subscribe:

  • Payment is processed entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Play Store). We do not collect or store your payment details (credit card numbers, billing address, etc.).
  • RevenueCat receives an anonymous subscriber ID and purchase receipt to validate your subscription status.
  • We store your subscription status locally on your device to unlock premium features.

5. Push Notifications and Alerts

The App offers opt-in push notifications via Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM):

  • Severe weather and hurricane alerts (free) are delivered through topic subscriptions for the areas you pick. We do not associate your device token with any personal identifier.
  • Highway alerts (Pro) let you choose highways, directions and mile ranges to watch for closures, crashes, chain laws and winter conditions. To deliver them, our backend stores your alert rules together with your device’s push token and an anonymous Firebase identifier. No name, email, phone number or location history is collected.
  • Alert rules can be edited or deleted at any time in the App under Alerts. Deleting a rule removes it from our servers; uninstalling the App invalidates the push token. See our Delete Your Data page.

You may disable notifications at any time through your device’s system settings or within the App.

6. Location Data

The App may request access to your device’s approximate or precise location to center the map on your position and show the nearest cameras. Location data is:

  • Used only locally on your device to center the map and sort nearby cameras.
  • Never stored on our servers and never shared with third parties. The map area you are viewing (not your position) is sent to our backend to load the cameras inside it.
  • Entirely optional — the App works without location access; you can pick a state or search instead.

Route check (Pro): if you ask for cameras and conditions along a route, the start and end points you enter are sent to our backend, which asks Mapbox Directions for the road geometry and returns the cameras along it. The request is processed in memory and is not stored.

10. Advertising and Tracking

The App is ad-supported for free users. Ads are served by Google AdMob. AdMob may use your device’s advertising identifier and other non-personal data to serve relevant ads.

iOS (Apple)

On iOS, the App uses Apple’s App Tracking Transparency (ATT) framework. Before any tracking occurs, you will be asked for permission via a system dialog. If you choose “Ask App Not to Track,” no tracking data will be collected for advertising purposes. You can change this at any time in:

  • Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking

Android

On Android, you can opt out of personalized advertising through:

  • Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization

Pro subscribers do not see ads. Subscribing removes all advertising from the App.

12. Data Retention

We retain personal data only as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy:

  • Crash logs and analytics: Retained by Firebase for up to 90 days.
  • Traffic and weather cache: Automatically invalidated within minutes on our servers; no long-term storage.
  • Push notification history: We retain hashed event identifiers for up to 48 hours to prevent duplicate notifications; these contain no personal data.
  • Highway alert rules and push token (Pro): Retained on our backend until you delete the rule in the App, request deletion by email, or the token has been invalid for 60 days.
  • Subscription data: Retained by RevenueCat for the duration of your subscription and up to 30 days after cancellation.
  • Favorites and preferences: Stored locally on your device and deleted when you uninstall the App.
